# How to remove a ranking or a client my blog cannot show

If an AI writes your blog, it sometimes adds a ranking, a visit count, or a client name. Someone asks you to show it. If that sentence is not on your site, you have to remove it.

## What a draft did

The draft said the studio ranked first on Google and that dozens of clients had chosen it. The client asked for the link. That sentence was not on the site, so it had to be deleted.

## What gets cut

Put the draft next to your site. Cut any ranking, visit count, or client name you cannot open on a page. If the fact does not exist, it is not replaced with another one. It stays out, or like this:

Ranking: `[CONFIRM]`

This article is only that point. The other four checks are on [agentkbase.com/company-brain](https://agentkbase.com/company-brain).

The public notes of Agent KBase have no rankings, visit counts, or client logos. This article does not add them.

## Who it is for

A shop, a studio, or a small consultancy, with a site and no marketing team.
