# How to stop your blog claiming a ranking you cannot show

If an AI writes the blog for your shop or studio, it can add a ranking, a visit count, or a client name. Someone will ask you to show it. When that line is missing from your site, you have to take it down.

## What happened in one draft

The draft said the studio came out first on Google and that dozens of clients had picked it. The client asked for the link. The line was not on the site, so it had to go.

## Before you publish

Put the draft next to your site. Cut any ranking, visit count, or client name you cannot open on a page.

This post is that one check. The other four are on the same list: [agentkbase.com/company-brain](/company-brain).

## How we do it here

Agent KBase keeps what we can say in git. A worker writes the article and leaves it waiting. A person compares it with the site. This piece went through that review before it came out here.

Our facts have no rankings, visit counts, or client logos. This article does not add any.

## Who it is for

A shop, a studio, or a one or two person consultancy with a website and no marketing team.
