# Service level

**Scope:** Free **agentkbase.com** website, documentation, and web wizard. Paid content and ads workers, Act connectors, done-for-you delivery, or private hosting are covered by separate agreements when offered.

## Target availability

We target **best-effort** availability for the public site. There is no financial credit for downtime on the free tier.

Planned maintenance may occur with little notice. We rely on Vercel for hosting; see [Status](https://www.vercel-status.com/) for platform incidents.

## What is included

* Static and server-rendered pages (learn docs, wizard UI, waitlist API when configured)
* Wizard export in the browser (no uptime guarantee for third-party npm registry)

## What is excluded

* LLM extract or other server LLM routes on the **public** deployment (intentionally disabled)
* Uptime of GitHub, npm, Cursor, SkillTree, Resend, or your own infrastructure
* Correctness of agent outputs in your repos

## Support

Community support via GitHub issues. Commercial support may be offered with paid products.
