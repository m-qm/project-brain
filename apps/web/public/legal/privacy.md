# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 2026

## Site operator

**agentkbase.com** is run by the **individual maintainer** of the Agent KBase open source project ([github.com/m-qm/project-brain](https://github.com/m-qm/project-brain)). There is **no separate registered legal entity** named on this site yet. For privacy requests and contact: **hello@agentkbase.com**.

If paid products launch under a company name later, this page and checkout terms will be updated with the legal name, address, and data controller details.

This policy covers the **website and hosted wizard**, not code you run locally or brains you store in your own git repos.

## What we collect

* **Waitlist and contact:** If you submit the content and ads workers waitlist or email us, we receive the address you provide and basic metadata (timestamp, source page). Notifications are sent via [Resend](https://resend.com) to the site operator.
* **Onboarding guide:** If you ask for the one-page guide, we store that email in Resend Contacts. You join the mailing list only if you tick that box. Without the tick, the address is kept for the request and marked unsubscribed, so it does not receive the list.
* **Analytics:** We use [Vercel Web Analytics](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics) on this site for aggregated page views and custom events (for example wizard export or waitlist submit). The default setup does not use advertising cookies.
* **Server logs:** Hosting providers may log IP addresses, user agents, and request paths for security and debugging.

We do **not** sell personal data. We do not run third-party ad trackers on agentkbase.com.

## What we do not collect on the public site

* Your **brain/** content from the wizard is processed in your browser session for export; we do not operate a hosted vault of your company facts on the free public tier.
* We do **not** enable shared LLM API keys on the public site. URL extract and other LLM features belong on your machine (CLI with your key) or a private deploy you control.

## Legal bases (EEA visitors)

Where GDPR applies, we process waitlist and guide email on **consent** (you submit the form). The mailing list is a separate tick. **Legitimate interest** covers security logs and aggregate analytics.

## Retention

Waitlist notices live in operator email until removed on request. Guide emails live in Resend Contacts until you ask us to delete them, or you unsubscribe from the list. Analytics aggregates are retained per Vercel’s product terms.

## Your rights

You may ask for access, correction, or deletion of waitlist or contact data by emailing **hello@agentkbase.com**. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

We may update this page. Material changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date.
