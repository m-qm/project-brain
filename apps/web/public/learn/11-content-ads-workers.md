# Content and ads workers

Goal: understand the **paid Act layer** without mixing it up with the OSS brain.

The CLI, wizard, and `@agentkbase/mcp` stay **read-only truth in git**. They do not post, boost, or message anyone.

## What you are buying (when it ships)

A company brain the agents must not contradict, plus connectors that only run after a human says yes:

1. **WhatsApp** — draft in, approve or reject out.
2. **Blog** — article on the company’s site only when `approved`. First loop for Agent KBase itself.
3. **LinkedIn** — draft from a published article. Not from pending text.

Instagram and Meta Ads are later formats of the same approved piece. Video is not the first test.

Quality is the product: sources, `[CONFIRM]` gaps, human review, no scaled sludge. Reach and rankings are not a deliverable.

## What this is not

* Not a Zapier catalogue (no Shopify, HubSpot, or CRM pack here).
* Not an auto-poster. Unapproved drafts stay in the queue.
* Not “we make you viral”.
* Not a request for invoices or passwords.
