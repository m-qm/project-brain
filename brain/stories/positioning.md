# Positioning (public-safe)

## Problem

Buyers confuse “company brain + 24/7 workers” (hosted, many connectors) with “folder of what you may say on your site.”

## How the offer works

Agent KBase fills `brain/` from a public URL (wizard or CLI), human crosses out inventions, `brain eval` catches draft prices/claims, human approves before live marketing. Optional done-for-you loop on company-brain.

## Wedge sentence

We do not sync the whole company — we make sure what you publish about **what you sell** matches what is true, with sources and your yes. **Your brain is files you own;** hosted Claude profile is optional entry, not the only store.
