# The knowledge layer every agent reads

For a studio or a one or two person consultancy whose website already describes the services. Shops, with prices and packs on the page, are a later test.

Goal: a knowledge base you recognise as your business. An agent, an article, or a LinkedIn post may only say what that base contains. If a line is not in it, the line is cut or marked for you to confirm. A person says yes before anything goes live.

This is not a similarity search. The product does not look up a “close enough” paragraph and hope. The sentence is in your files, or it waits on `[CONFIRM]`, or it is gone.

## 1. Paste the site

Open the [wizard](/wizard). Paste the public URL of your studio. Extract a draft.

The draft is a guess from your page: services, who you serve, and what the page actually says. It is not a new offer.

## 2. Cross out what you do not sell

Read the draft next to your site. Delete any service, pack, price, or deadline that is not on the page and that you will not honour.

If the page says “on request” and the draft says a number, the number goes. A fluent sentence is not a fact.

## 3. Write who you serve, and what you will not say

In the facts, keep one answer for each:

* Who the work is for.
* What you will not claim: rankings, traffic, client names, or results you cannot show on a public page.

Leave gaps as `[CONFIRM]`. Do not fill them to make the file look finished.

## 4. Ask something a client would ask

Use a question a real client sends. “What do you do, and what does it cost?” is enough.

The answer is valid only when every claim is in the base you just edited, or you are looking at a `[CONFIRM]` you still have to answer. If the reply adds a client, a ranking, or a price that is not there, the base is not ready.

## 5. Every agent reads that base

If you can say “this describes my business”, that file is the base. The agent that answers a client, the one that writes an article, and the one that drafts a LinkedIn post all read it. A piece for the site or for LinkedIn comes after, and only with your yes.

The checks for anything that would go live are on [the knowledge layer](/company-brain). We do not promise new clients, Google rankings, or hours saved.

## What this guide does not open

One question first: what you sell. Marketing, sales, and operations are not a second product. Transversal means every agent reads this same base, not that every department is in scope on day one.
