# Project facts — Agent KBase

## Mission

Open-source **agent knowledge base**: curated business truth in git (`brain/`), plus CLI, web wizard, read-only MCP, and remote MCP (Streamable HTTP on the web app). Optional Engram for decisions across chats.

**Commercial layer (separate from open core):** done-for-you setup, draft review, publish workflow with human approval — quoted after a start call. Auto-publish connectors (WhatsApp, blog, LinkedIn Act) are waitlist / not shipped in OSS.

## ICP

Self-employed or small company with a **public website** and **no marketing team** (shop, studio, consultancy).

## Ground truth

* **Draft rule:** A price, client, ranking, or result not in facts is cut or marked `[CONFIRM]`; nothing goes live without human yes.
* **Service flow (described, not fully automated in v1):** draft → human approve or stop → article on site only after yes → LinkedIn only after article is live.
* **Five pre-publish checks** on https://agentkbase.com/company-brain (title, prices in notes, provable claims, traceability, safe CTA).
* **Not similarity RAG:** the product does not answer from “close enough” text; claims must be in the folder or `[CONFIRM]`.

## Do not invent

Anything not listed here or in sibling fact files must be confirmed with the project owner.
