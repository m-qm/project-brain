# Offer limits (what we do not promise)

## Public commitments we refuse in copy

* Guaranteed Google rankings, traffic, or “#1 in AI answers” (GEO).
* New client volume or hours saved as a deliverable.
* Auto-publish without human approval on the OSS path.
* A hosted-only brain with no export to files you own (we offer both: hosted session + `brain.zip` / git).

## Positioning wedge

We govern **what a small business may publish as true** (site → brain → eval → human yes), not full company ops (CRM, Slack ingestion, 24/7 workers). Competitors such as GuruSup target scaleups with connectors and workers; we target marketing truth for owners with a website.

## Indexing stance (Phase 1)

* Facts live in `brain/facts/` with stable headings.
* Learn guides are **reference** under `brain/stories/learn/` (synced); agents distill into facts before treating as ground truth.
* Future claim index is **derived** from approved markdown — index does not approve new facts.
