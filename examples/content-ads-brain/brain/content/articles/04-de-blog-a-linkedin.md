# How to reuse a blog article on LinkedIn without a new offer

The article is approved. LinkedIn still wants a post today. The risk is writing a second piece that quietly changes the pack.

People search this when they already have a blog draft and do not want two versions of the offer in the world.

## The problem

The article said “price on request”. The LinkedIn post, written in a hurry, says “from this month”. A lead replies to LinkedIn. You never sold that. You now argue with your own post.

Distribution is not a new brief. It is the same facts, shorter.

## What does not work

A prompt that says “make this more viral”. A carousel with a new bonus. A hook that invents a deadline to create urgency. Scheduling LinkedIn from pending text the article agent has not cleared.

If the article is not published, LinkedIn stays idle. That is the point.

## What does

1. Wait until the article is approved and live (or at least marked published in the queue).
2. Pull the problem, the five checks, and the CTA from that text.
3. Write ES first, then EN. Same offer. No extra proof.
4. Run the same five yeses on the caption.
5. A person pastes. The worker does not log into LinkedIn.

## How we run it here

The LinkedIn agent may read only a `published` article. It writes `brain/content/linkedin/` and leaves a pending item `li-article-NN`. First readers come from here, not from Google.

We do not promise impressions. We promise the caption cannot invent a pack the article already forbade.

## Who this is for

The same founder who approved the article. If someone else posts, they still run the five checks.
