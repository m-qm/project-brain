# How to stop your blog claiming a ranking you cannot show

If you run a shop or a studio and you let an AI write the blog, the expensive line is not a clumsy sentence. It is a ranking, a visit count, or a client name you cannot open on a page and show.

A shop owner is not searching “how to measure SEO”. They are searching how to publish a post that does not invent proof. That is buying intent. This answers that search.

## The problem (yours, not the builder’s)

A realistic example, not a result of ours: the draft says the studio is “top on Google” and that “40 clients chose us”. A customer asks where that is. Your site does not say it. Your notes do not say it. You now have to walk the sentence back, or pretend you meant it.

The model filled a gap where the proof should have been. Fluency is not a screenshot.

## What is not enough

Telling it “only write true claims” is not enough. A style pass is not enough. A footer that says “results may vary” is not enough: the false line is already in the post your network will quote.

A tool that publishes alone scales the same sentence to the next ten posts.

## What works: cut the proof you cannot show

Before the text goes live, someone marks this yes or cuts the line:

No traffic, rankings, or client names you cannot show. If the proof is not on your site, or in the facts you keep, it does not go in the draft.

The other four checks sit on the same list. This post is only this one. The full list is public: [agentkbase.com/company-brain](/company-brain).

## How we run it here

Agent KBase keeps what is true in git. One worker writes the article and leaves it pending. A human compares the proof lines with the site. Nothing in this draft is a ranking, a visit count, or a client logo of ours, because those facts are not in the brain. We do not promise Google rankings.

This file is a pending draft. It is not on the site until that review is yes.

## Who this is for

A shop, a studio, or a one or two person consultancy with a website and no marketing team. Not a department that already has an agency.
