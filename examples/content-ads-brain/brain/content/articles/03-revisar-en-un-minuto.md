# How to review an AI draft in one minute

If you are the only person who can say yes, the system stops at your calendar. A one-minute list is the product. A meeting is not.

Founders search this after the first bad draft: how to check what the model wrote without losing the morning.

## The problem

You open the draft to “fix the tone”. Tone is cheap. The expensive line is a price, a pack, or a deadline that is not on your site. If review means reading every sentence for style, you will skip it. Then something goes live that a client can quote.

## The five yeses

Approve only if every line is yes. If one is no, the draft stays in the queue.

1. The title is a shop or studio problem, not a jargon word.
2. Every price, pack, or deadline is on your site. If it is not, it is cut.
3. No rankings, traffic, or client names you cannot prove.
4. The draft says where each fact came from: your site or a public page.
5. The ask is the waitlist, the site, or the brand WhatsApp. Never invoices, passwords, or a personal phone.

That list is public: [agentkbase.com/company-brain](/company-brain).

## How to fail this

Reviewing for “whether it sounds like us”. Adding a sixth check every week. Approving from your phone without opening the live offer page. Letting the model publish when you are busy.

Busy is when the false pack ships.

## How we run it here

The article agent must leave the draft pending. A human runs the five checks. Only then does the piece move. LinkedIn is a second queue, from the published article, with the same five yeses.

We do not promise that review stays one minute if you add extra gates. The bottleneck is you. Design the list so you can finish it.

## Who this is for

The person who will actually click approve. Usually the founder. Not a future marketing hire.
