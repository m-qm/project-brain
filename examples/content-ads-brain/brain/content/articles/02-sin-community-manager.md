# Who writes your weekly post if you have no marketing hire

You have a website. You know what you sell. Friday comes and the blog is still last month. You do not need a community manager first. You need one true list of the offer, a draft, and a one-minute yes.

That is the buying question. Not “how to hire a content team”.

## The problem

Silence feels safer than a post that invents a pack. So the founder waits. A freelancer who never saw the offer fills the gap with something that sounds like you. A model does the same, faster. A client quotes it. You did not mean to sell it.

The cost is not “inconsistent branding”. The cost is a promise you now have to honour or walk back.

## What does not work

Posting every day from a prompt. Auto-publish to WordPress. Waiting until you “have time to write”. Hiring someone only to publish, before anyone can check the offer.

A tool that posts alone scales the error.

## What does

1. Write down what is true: services, prices you will honour, what you will not say.
2. Answer one buying question in one article.
3. Run five checks that fit in a minute. They are on [agentkbase.com/company-brain](/company-brain).
4. A person says yes.
5. Only then does LinkedIn get a draft of that same text. Not a new offer.

You still approve. You do not have to draft from a blank page every week.

## How we run it here

Agent KBase keeps the offer in git. The article agent writes the next row on the slate and leaves it pending. The LinkedIn agent cannot start until that article is published. Nothing fires alone.

This site is the case study. We do not promise Google traffic. The first readers come from conversations and LinkedIn.

## Who this is for

A shop, studio, or one or two person consultancy with a site and no marketing team. Not a department that already has an agency.
