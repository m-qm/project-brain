# Content pillars

Four repeating themes. The product, not a person.

1. **Truth before fluency.** The model writes well. The brain is what stops it inventing the offer.
2. **HITL is the product.** A worker that publishes without review is an incident.
3. **One approved piece, then distribute.** LinkedIn comes from a published article, not a second invented story.
4. **Tools are not a brain.** Skills say what the agent can do. Facts say what is true.

Formats: article first, LinkedIn from that article. Instagram, Reels, and ads are later formats of the same approved text.

CTA on every piece: agentkbase.com/company-brain or the brand WhatsApp. Never a personal number. Never “upload invoices”.
