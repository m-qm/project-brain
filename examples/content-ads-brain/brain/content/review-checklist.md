# Review checklist (5 points)

Approve only if every line is yes. If any line is no, reject or edit the brain and re-queue.

1. **Buyer's problem.** The title could be searched by a shop or studio owner. Not a builder term (MCP, RAG, “agent”).
2. **No invented offer.** Every price, pack, deadline, or “from €” is in `brain/facts/` or was cut.
3. **No invented proof.** No rankings, traffic, ROAS, client names, or user counts unless they are in facts.
4. **Sources.** The draft lists the brain files (or public pages) it used.
5. **Safe CTA.** Waitlist, site, or brand WhatsApp. No invoices, passwords, or personal phone.
