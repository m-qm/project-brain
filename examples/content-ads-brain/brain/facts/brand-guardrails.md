# Brand guardrails

These rules apply to every article, LinkedIn draft, Reel, and WhatsApp reply.

## Never

* A person's name, face, voice, or city
* Employers, job titles, or CV dates
* “We make you viral” or follower guarantees
* “Send us your invoices” or any ask for billing PDFs
* A personal mobile number
* DualPerspective OS as a live SaaS you can log into today
* Metrics that are not in `facts/`

## Always

* Product voice (“we”, Agent KBase)
* CTA: site, waitlist, or the brand WhatsApp (eSIM), never a personal line
* HITL: draft then approve. Run the 5-point checklist in `content/review-checklist.md` first.
* Titles speak to a shop or studio owner, not to a builder.
* If unsure, mark `[CONFIRM]` and stop
