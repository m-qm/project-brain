# Project facts — Agent KBase proof account

## Mission

Public, faceless content loop that shows how a git brain plus two workers (article and LinkedIn distribution) ship with human approval. agentkbase.com is the case study. The account teaches the method. It does not sell a person's story.

## Ground truth

* Product name on the site: Agent KBase. Site: https://agentkbase.com
* OSS layer is free: CLI, wizard, read-only MCP (`brain_brief`, `brain_validate`, `brain_read`).
* First loop is blog + LinkedIn, not Instagram or ads. Video is a later format of an approved article.
* Paid layer (not shipped in OSS): WhatsApp HITL, site/blog publish after `approved`, LinkedIn draft after the article is published.
* Done-for-you is the first paid motion. Act connectors for agencies come after the operated loop is proven.
* Quality gate is the product: sources from the brain, `[CONFIRM]` on gaps, human approve, no scaled sludge.
* Viral reach and SEO rankings are not KPIs we invent. A new blog can take months; LinkedIn and the waitlist carry distribution in the meantime.
* First paying ICP: founder-led company with a website and no marketing team (serious autónomo or pyme, not an in-house marketing department).
* No invoices, passwords, or personal phone numbers are collected for this loop.
* Language: English on agentkbase.com. LinkedIn drafts ES and EN.
* Voice: product “we”. Never a founder, city, employer, or CV.

## Do not invent

Anything not listed above must be confirmed with the operator before you state it as fact. No follower counts, no ROAS, no client logos, no invented Google rankings.
