# Customer discovery — Company Brain Pack (5–8 calls)

Use after sharing the [Company Brain scope](COMPANY-BRAIN-SCOPE.md) one-pager. Goal: validate **pack vs done-for-you** and price bands before building templates.

## Segments

| Segment | Where to find | Hook |
|---------|---------------|------|
| SkillTree audit alumni | Past clients, waitlist | “Turn audit into git ground truth” |
| Wizard users | Vercel Analytics on `/wizard`, `wizard_export` | “Beyond starter brain.zip” |
| Vibecoders / small SaaS | Indie Hackers, local communities, X | “Agents that know your ops, not just your README” |

## Screener (2 min)

* Do you use Cursor (or similar) on a product repo?
* Is there a public marketing site or internal Notion/docs?
* Who would edit ground truth (founder only vs team)?

## Core questions

1. Walk me through the last time an agent **invented** something wrong (metric, feature, policy).
2. Where does “company truth” live today (README, Notion, heads)?
3. If you got a **zip in your repo** with marketing, sales, ops, fulfillment facts plus validation, what would you do in week one?
4. **Magic wand:** SkillTree audit + brain in git — what must be included for you to pay?
5. **Price test (randomize order):**
   * Self-serve pack **€49** — templates + guide, you fill.
   * Self-serve pack **€149** — above + video walkthrough + validate rules.
   * Done-for-you **€499** — we interview 90 min, deliver validated zip.
   * Done-for-you **€1,200** — audit handoff + 30 day Slack Q&A on brain edits.
6. Which matters more: **owning files in git** or **fastest time to first agent win**?
7. Would you buy **maintenance** (quarterly brain review) within 6 months? At what price?

## Signals

| Strong buy (pack or DFY) | Weak / no |
|---------------------------|-----------|
| Past agent failures cost time or client trust | “We just use custom instructions” |
| Already uses git + Cursor rules | Wants hosted Notion brain only |
| SkillTree or audit mindset | No budget; student project |
